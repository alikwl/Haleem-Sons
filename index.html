About Haleem Private Limited

Haleem Private Limited is the most trusted and established dealer of Millat Tractors and Massey Ferguson in Khanewal, serving the agricultural community of South Punjab for over 15 years.

Our commitment to quality, fair pricing, and exceptional after-sales service has made us the first choice for farmers and agricultural businesses across the region. We provide end-to-end solutions — from helping you choose the right tractor to delivering it to your doorstep with full warranty coverage.

What We Offer

Our Products & Services

Millat Tractors

Pakistan's most popular and reliable tractor brand. Available in MF 240, MF 260, MF 350, MF 375, MF 385 and more — built for every farming need.

Massey Ferguson

Internationally renowned brand with AGCO backing. Known for exceptional build quality, fuel efficiency, and long-lasting performance in the toughest conditions.

Agricultural Equipment
